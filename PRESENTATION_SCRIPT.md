# EightMinute — Website Presentation Script

**Your slot:** end of the team presentation
**Target length:** 1 min 30 sec – 2 min
**Your job:** show the website live + conclude

> Tip: Have the site already open in the browser **before** you start talking. Have two things ready: the **landing page** and the **challenge form** (in a second tab). Don't fumble with loading — keep it smooth.

---

## THE SCRIPT

### 1. Open — the landing page (~20 sec)
> *(Show: landing page / hero — "8 Minutes. One Challenge. Real Impact.")*

"So, to actually bring this campaign to life, I built the website that powers it.

This is the home page. The whole idea mirrors the ice-bucket challenge — you take one of three 8-minute challenges, you reflect on your mental health, and then you pass it on. Everything here is designed to make the movement feel **alive**."

---

### 2. Show it's live — stats + growth (~20 sec)
> *(Scroll down slowly to: Live Challenge Counts + the growth chart)*

"And it's not just static. These counters and this chart update in real time — so as people take the challenge, you literally watch the movement grow. That live feedback is what keeps a viral campaign going."

---

### 3. The actual challenge (~30 sec)
> *(Switch to the challenge form tab. Click a challenge — e.g. Ice Bath. Then scroll the 8 questions.)*

"Here's the core of it. You pick your challenge — ice bath, treadmill, or hot wings — and then you answer **8 short questions** about mental health, one at a time.

This is the part that matters. Every person who takes part actually stops and reflects. And at the end, you **nominate someone else** — that's what keeps the chain going."

> *(Scroll to the nominate section to show the nominate fields, then optionally hit submit.)*

---

### 4. The payoff — the live feed (~20 sec)
> *(Back to the landing page, scroll to "Recent Participants" feed.)*

"And every submission lands here, on a public feed, where anyone can **like and comment** — so it becomes a community, not just a form. This is how 8 minutes turns into real, shared impact."

---

### 5. Conclude (~15–20 sec)
> *(Stop clicking. Face the audience.)*

"So that's EightMinute — one platform that brings our whole campaign together.

It all comes back to one idea: giving someone just **8 minutes** of your time can genuinely help them through a tough moment. We took that 8-minute rule, tied it to the number 8 that Brent Pope wore on his back, and turned it into a challenge built to spread like the ice-bucket challenge — take the challenge, reflect, and pass it on.

Because the goal isn't really the ice bath or the hot wings. It's getting people to finally talk about the elephant in the room — mental health. Thank you."

---

## Delivery reminders
- **Slow down on the scrolls** — let people see the screen.
- If a click lags, keep talking; don't go silent waiting for it.
- Don't read the 8 questions aloud — just gesture at them ("8 reflective questions").
- End on eye contact, not on the screen.
